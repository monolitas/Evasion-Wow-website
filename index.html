01 / A shared world

Beyond the
familiar paths.

A distant cavern. An unexpected encounter. Another party’s torchlight in the dark. Even familiar places hold something uncertain.

You are not alone down there.

The depths belong to everyone. The strangers you meet might share the journey—or stand between your party and what you came to find.

Something worth finding.

A hard-earned weapon. A rare material. Something made by another player. The things you carry should have a story behind them.

Stay a little longer.

Gather your friends, venture further and learn a place together. There is more to a journey than reaching the last room.

Exploration feels different when you don’t know who you’ll meet.

02 / Rivalry & consequence

Pick your fights.
Live with your choices.

An ally today. A rival tomorrow. A name you remember.

An encounter

A moment to choose.

Not every stranger is an enemy. Share the road, keep your distance, or find a reason to trust.

A rivalry

Stand your ground.

A contested hunt can become a fight your guild talks about long after everyone has gone home.

A reputation

Choices leave a mark.

Taking a fight to someone who didn’t ask for one comes with risk. What you do matters beyond the moment.

Victory is sweeter when something is at stake.

And sometimes the best thing you bring back is the person who stayed beside you.

03 / More than a name

Find your people.
Raise your banner.

The late-night hunts. The narrow escapes. The friends who come when you call. That is what makes a guild.

Build something together.

Your guild grows through shared effort. What you earn together becomes part of what you can take on next.

Rivals worth remembering.

A rival banner on the horizon changes the evening. Old grudges, unexpected alliances and battles give each guild its own history.

A place to call yours.

Town sieges bring that rivalry home. Rally around your banner, fight for a foothold and give your guild a place on the map.

Town sieges are still being tested as the world takes shape.

Server features

What’s different
in Evasion Worlds.

Custom PvP, shared dungeons and guild-controlled towns. Features are being tested and balanced during closed alpha.

Shared dungeons & raids
No private party instances. Groups share farming areas and compete for mobs, bosses and loot.
Dungeon farming
Improved dungeon gold and item drops. Bosses return on randomized 2–5-hour timers.
Open-world PvP & karma
Ctrl-attack neutral players. PvP flags turn purple; unlawful kills add karma and turn names red.
PK equipment drops
Red players can lose items on death. Anyone can loot their grave.
Guild progression
Earn guild experience and unlock shared guild skills.
Clan wars
Guilds agree to war. War kills do not add karma or PK points.
Town sieges & taxes
Capture towns, gain loyal guards and collect local NPC transaction taxes. Currently in testing.
Crafting & trade
No bind-on-equip restriction. Equipment drops are uncommon, giving crafting more value.
Craftable detection wards
Place destructible wards that reveal nearby concealed players without removing their stealth.
