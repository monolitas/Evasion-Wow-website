WE ARE NOT MAKING ANOTHER CONVENIENCE REALM.

The world itself is the content.

Systems are being bent back toward one goal: keep players visible, keep danger meaningful, and make encounters with other people part of progression instead of something hidden behind a queue.

I · FACTIONLESS

No permanent sides.

Group, trade, guild and cooperate across the old Horde and Alliance boundary. Friends and enemies are defined by what happens in the world.

Cross-faction groupsCross-faction guildsShared economy
II · DANGER

Karma PvP.

Start fights if you choose. Killing unwilling players pushes your karma toward outlaw status.

III · PRESENCE

Open dungeons.

Dungeon spaces remain part of the real world. Other groups can enter, contest routes or turn a pull into a fight.

IV · CONFLICT

World raid bosses.

Major bosses are public resources. Scouting, mobilisation, politics and area control matter as much as execution.

FLAGGING & KARMA

Attack anyone.
Own the consequence.

Evasion separates consensual fighting from murder. Danger is open; griefing is not consequence-free.
