01 / The world

Server features.

Custom systems built around shared exploration, player trade and guild competition. Balance is evolving during closed alpha.

01

Shared dungeons & raids

No private party instances. Groups share farming areas and compete for mobs, bosses and loot.

02

Dungeon farming

Improved dungeon gold and item drops. Bosses return on randomized 2–5-hour timers.

03

Crafting & trade

No bind-on-equip restriction. Uncommon equipment drops give player crafting and trading more value.

04

Detection wards

Craft and place destructible wards to reveal nearby concealed players without removing their stealth.

02 / Conflict

PvP with consequences.

Player names show their PvP state. Choose your fights carefully: unlawful kills carry a risk.

Neutral

Not flagged for PvP.

Hold Ctrl to attack a neutral player. Unlawful kills add karma and PK points.

Flagged

Purple name.

A purple name identifies a PvP-flagged player. Check a player’s state before attacking.

PK / Karma

Red name. Equipment at risk.

Red players can lose items on death. Their grave can be looted by other players.

03 / Your guild

Progress. Fight.
Claim a town.

Guild systems give your group shared progression, rivalries and territory to defend.

I

Guild progression

Earn guild experience and unlock shared guild skills.

II

Clan wars

Guilds agree to war. War kills do not add karma or PK points.

III

Town sieges

Register your guild, fight for the banner and capture a settlement.

IV

Ownership & taxes

The owning guild gains loyal guards and collects local NPC transaction taxes.
