01 / A shared world

You won’t be
farming alone.

No private copy waiting behind the dungeon entrance. Your party enters the same place as everyone else.

Familiar places. Different rules.

Dungeons and raids become shared farming grounds. Rival groups may cooperate, compete for a boss, or settle things themselves.

A reason to stay.

Dungeon farming offers better returns, while equipment remains uncommon. Crafting matters. Gathering spots deplete. Bosses return on long, randomized timers.

Earn your claim.

Damage decides initial monster-loot ownership, not the first hit. Certain scripted bosses are called with rare items found in their dungeon.

Shared maps are implemented. Individual encounters and large-group performance are still being tested.

02 / Rivalry & consequence

Pick your fights.
Live with your choices.

Your name tells a story before you say a word.

White

Neutral

Not looking for a fight. Force-attacking a neutral player requires Ctrl.

Purple

In the fight

Taking part in PvP flags you. Other players can attack you freely.

Red

Karma

Killing an innocent player has consequences. On death, equipped gear and inventory items can be lost.

Red-player drops go into a public grave. No Loot All—each item is a race.

Party protection and clan-war rules make the distinction between a rival and an ally matter.

03 / More than a name

Build a guild.
Make a rival.

Guild membership is a shared progression track, not just another chat channel.

Grow together.

Guild experience and spendable skill points are separate. Leaders unlock shared skills using guild-bank gold and special materials.

Wars that mean something.

Clan wars require both sides to agree, and peace does too. Enemy guilds are clearly marked. War kills do not add Karma or lifetime PK points.

A town worth taking.

The Crossroads siege system includes a capture banner, loyal guards, rally standards and town taxes. Earned treasury balances remain with the guild that earned them.

Sieges are under controlled testing. Production sieges remain disabled until validation is complete.

Which client do I need?

World of Warcraft 3.3.5a, build 12340, enUS. The launcher checks the executable and verifies the Evasion files before play.

Can I download the launcher here?

Not yet. The launcher is built, but its PC-hosted download feed and outside access still need configuring. Invited testers receive instructions directly from the host.

Will my progress survive the alpha?

Don’t treat alpha progress as permanent. Testing may require resets, balance changes or data recovery.

Are all these systems finished?

No. Implemented means the code is in place, not that every encounter, edge case or large battle has passed live testing.
