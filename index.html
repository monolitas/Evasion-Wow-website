Realm identity

The world comes first.

Evasion is not built around queueing into isolated content. It is built around travel, discovery, tension, grudges, rivalries, and that strange beautiful feeling that the whole world is listening when something important happens.

One connected world

Every system feeds the next story.

When no faction wall exists, social choices matter more. When dungeons and raid bosses live in the world, conflict gains geography, timing, politics, and memory.

PvP with consequences

Anyone can fight.
Not every kill is the same.

Evasion separates mutual combat from predation. Defend yourself, clash with willing enemies, or choose the darker road and accept the consequences that follow.

Neutral

Traveling, farming, or passing through the world without active PvP involvement.

  • Not currently flagged for combat
  • Can group, explore, and farm normally
  • Being killed unfairly matters

Flagged

The temporary combat state created by starting or joining a fight.

  • Marks active PvP participation
  • Mutual fights carry far less stigma
  • Signals recent combat involvement

Criminal

The price of repeatedly killing neutral or unwilling players.

  • Negative karma and social exposure
  • Becomes valid prey for others
  • Creates bounty, pressure, and risk
✦

Reputation is world content

The point is not random chaos. The point is memory. Names become meaningful when the world remembers who fights fair, who preys on the weak, and who turns every boss spawn into a war.

Open world endgame

No instances.
No private victories.

Dungeons and raid bosses remain exposed to the world. That means scouting, pathing, interference, defense, opportunism, and stories that cannot happen inside sealed copies.

Open dungeons

Shared progression space

Dungeon entrances, chokepoints, route control, and room pressure become part of the actual game instead of disappearing behind portals.

  • Groups can meet each other naturally
  • Contested pulls create emergent conflict
  • Scouting and timing matter again
World raid bosses

Guild conflict with real stakes

Boss control becomes public. Guilds need information, organization, defense, and reputation to secure the kill.

  • Bosses create real rally moments
  • Opposition can contest the encounter
  • Victories become remembered events

Philosophy

“The best stories are the ones the world did not promise you.”

Evasion is built for players who miss uncertainty: the long road, the remembered enemy, the strange alliance, the interrupted dungeon run, the boss call that turns into a war, and the feeling that reputation matters even after the fight ends.
